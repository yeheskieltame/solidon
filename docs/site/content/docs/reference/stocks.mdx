The factory lists eight stocks, fixed at init. A creator picks one as the meme's thesis stock in `create_meme(thesis_stock)`; it cannot be changed. Read the list with `get_stocks` on `solidon.testnet`.

<Callout type="warn" title="Testnet tokens">
Every stock token on this page is synthetic: a Solidon-deployed token with a Solidon-seeded pool, priced from the real Chainlink feed. They are not tokenized shares and have no value.
</Callout>

## Pricing

<Steps>
<Step>

### Chainlink is the reference

The keeper reads `latestRoundData()` from the official Chainlink proxies on Robinhood Chain mainnet. These are 24/5 equity feeds with 8 decimals.

</Step>
<Step>

### The keeper writes the mock oracle

Every `ORACLE_INTERVAL_SECS` (300 s) the keeper calls `set_price(price_id, price, expo)` on `pyth.solidon.testnet` for all eight stocks in one NEAR transaction. The feed id is the Pyth id of `Equity.US.<TICKER>/USD`, used as the oracle key. A Chainlink answer older than 25 hours (weekend or broken feed) is not pushed.

</Step>
<Step>

### The contract reads one price per stock

The meme calls `get_price_no_older_than(price_id, 900)`. A price older than 15 minutes is stale: `rebalance`, `redeem`, `fund_chain` and the stock buys then do nothing. The same price values the stock held on every chain.

</Step>
<Step>

### Buys are bounded by the oracle

`buy_stock` and `buy_stock_solana` sign a swap with `minOut` = oracle value of the USDC in, minus 1%. If the pool fills worse than that, the swap reverts on the destination chain.

</Step>
</Steps>

The mock oracle is open on testnet (anyone can call `set_price`), so testnet mNAV numbers are a demo, not a market signal. See [mNAV](/docs/concepts/mnav).

## Tokens per chain

| Chain | Token standard | Symbol | Decimals | Venue | Pool fee | Cash proof |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Base Sepolia | ERC-20 (`MockERC20`) | `NVDAc`, `AAPLc`, ... | 8 | Uniswap v3 (SwapRouter02 `exactInputSingle`) | 0.3% | MPC-verified (`credit_cash`) |
| Robinhood Chain testnet | ERC-20 (`MockERC20`) | `NVDA`, `AAPL`, ... | 18 | Uniswap v3 (SwapRouter02 `exactInputSingle`) | 0.3% | Relayer (`report_cash`) |
| Solana devnet | Token-2022 mint with metadata | `NVDAx`, `AAPLx`, ... | 8 | Raydium CLMM (`swap_v2`) | 0.05% | Relayer (`report_cash`) |

Every listed stock has one venue on each of the three chains. Each token address, pool and explorer link is on [Deployments](/docs/reference/deployments).

USDC per chain: Circle USDC (6 decimals) on Base Sepolia, the testnet USDC (18 decimals) on Robinhood, Circle devnet USDC (6 decimals) on Solana. The contract normalises all of them to 6 decimals in NAV.

### Re-peg

One-sided liquidity starts at the reference price. When the stock falls, a buy still fills at the range edge, above the oracle, and the treasury's swap fails its `minOut`. The keeper's re-peg pass (every 30 minutes in production) moves any pool whose next buy fills more than 0.5% above Chainlink to 0.995 x Chainlink and adds $50,000 of new stock liquidity. A pool below the oracle is left alone. See [Keeper](/docs/reference/keeper).
