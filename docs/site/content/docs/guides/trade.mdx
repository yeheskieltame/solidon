Every meme trades against USDC in a constant-product pool inside its own contract. There is no external DEX. Prices, fees and the mNAV rules all live in the same contract.

On testnet the USDC is `usdc.solidon.testnet`, a mock token with 6 decimals and no value. Get 1,000 of it with **Get test funds** (see [Quickstart](/docs/quickstart)).

## Buy

Open a meme from **Markets**. The trade panel has a **Buy** tab and a **Sell** tab (and **Redeem** while the redemption window is open).

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| You pay | USDC amount. Prefilled with 10. **MAX** fills your whole balance |
| You get | Estimated tokens out, after the fee, from the pool's current reserves |
| Details → Minimum received | The least you accept. The contract refuses the trade below it |
| Details → Average price | USDC paid per token for this trade |
| Details → Price impact, fee included | Average price against the current spot price |
| Details → Fee | `1% (0.8% treasury, 0.2% creator)` |
| Details → Max slippage | 0.5%, 1% (default) or 3%. Sets Minimum received |
| Details → Network | NEAR testnet |

The button says exactly what happens: **Buy ~… SYMBOL for 10 USDC**. If something blocks the trade, the button says why instead: "Enter an amount", "Not enough USDC", "Amount too small".

After you confirm, a toast shows "Buying SYMBOL", then "Bought SYMBOL" with a link **The transaction on NearBlocks**. If you run out of USDC, the panel shows "Out of USDC? It is free on testnet." with a **Get test funds** button.

### What the buy transaction contains

A buy is one call on the USDC token, not on the meme:

```json title="ft_transfer_call on usdc.solidon.testnet (1 yoctoNEAR attached)"
{
  "receiver_id": "honey.solidon.testnet",
  "amount": "10000000",
  "msg": "{\"action\":\"buy\",\"min_out\":\"<minimum tokens, 18 decimals>\"}"
}
```

The USDC contract calls the meme's `ft_on_transfer`. The meme then:

1. Splits the 1% fee: 0.8% to treasury cash, 0.2% credited to the creator as claimable USDC.
2. Swaps the rest through the pool (`x * y = k`).
3. Refuses with "Slippage: output below min_out" if the output is below `min_out`. Any refusal refunds the full USDC through the token standard.
4. Sends the tokens to you and logs a `buy` event.

With your own wallet, the first buy of a meme also calls `storage_deposit` on the meme (0.00125 NEAR) so you can hold its token. The wallet asks twice. The demo wallet signs both without popups.

## Sell

On the **Sell** tab, enter an amount in "You sell" (or **MAX**). The panel shows "You get ≈ … USDC", with the same Details rows.

The button reads **Sell … SYMBOL for ~… USDC**. It calls `sell(amount, min_out)` on the meme with 1 yoctoNEAR attached. The contract:

1. Swaps your tokens through the pool.
2. Takes the same 1% fee from the USDC out (0.8% treasury, 0.2% creator).
3. Refuses if the net USDC is below `min_out`.
4. Sends you the USDC with `ft_transfer`.

The success toast says "Sold SYMBOL. USDC is on its way; if the transfer fails it becomes claimable." Claimable USDC appears in the trade panel as "… USDC waiting for you" with a **Claim … USDC** button, which calls `claim_usdc`.

## Pay from another chain

Under the Buy tab, **Pay from another chain** opens a NEAR Intents 1Click quote: any asset 1Click routes into USDC on NEAR, the asset a Solidon buy is paid in.

- Common assets are listed first: SOL, ETH on Base, ETH on Arbitrum, BTC, and USDC on Base, Solana and Arbitrum. **Search all assets** lists the rest.
- Enter an amount in "You send". About 500 ms after you stop typing, the app requests a quote and shows "You get ≈ … USDC on NEAR".
- Details show **Minimum received**, **Route cost**, **Arrives in** and **To**.

<Callout type="info" title="On testnet this is a preview">
The panel reads: "Mainnet route preview — on testnet use Get test funds. Quotes are live; no deposit address is issued and nothing is swapped." Quotes are real 1Click mainnet quotes requested with `dry: true`. A testnet account does not exist on mainnet, so the preview quotes to `intents.near` as a stand-in recipient.
</Callout>

On a mainnet build, the same panel offers **Get a … deposit address**, an optional refund address, and then follows the swap: "Waiting for your deposit", "Deposit seen, waiting for confirmations", "Swapping into USDC on NEAR", "Done: the USDC is in your NEAR account".

## Where the fees go

| Share | Destination | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0.8% | Treasury cash on NEAR | Raises NAV. Cash above the 20% buffer is sent to the treasury chains and buys stock |
| 0.2% | Creator, claimable USDC | Claimed with `claim_usdc` |

See [How it works](/docs/concepts/how-it-works) for how treasury cash becomes stock on Base, Robinhood Chain and Solana.
