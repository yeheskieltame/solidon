---

`rebalance()` runs a meme's mNAV rules. It is public: any NEAR account can call it, at most once per hour per meme, and the caller earns a small tip when the call moves value. No backend is needed for the rules to run. The Solidon keeper is one caller among many.

## What one call does

<Mermaid chart={`flowchart TD
  A[rebalance called] --> B{Cooldown over?}
  B -- no --> X[Panics: Rebalance cooldown has not ended]
  B -- yes --> C[Read oracle price, max age 15 min]
  C --> D{Fresh price?}
  D -- no --> S[stale_price: nothing runs, cooldown not used]
  D -- yes --> E{Pool TWAP and NAV available?}
  E -- no --> N[no_market: nothing runs, cooldown not used]
  E -- yes --> F[Compute mNAV = TWAP / NAV, start cooldown]
  F --> G{mNAV}
  G -- above 2.0x --> I[Issue new tokens, proceeds to treasury cash]
  G -- 1.0x to 2.0x --> H[no_action]
  G -- below 1.0x --> K[Buy back and burn with cash above the buffer]
  F --> R[Redemption window open if mNAV below 0.9x]`} />

In detail:

1. **Cooldown.** The call fails with "Rebalance cooldown has not ended" until one hour has passed since the last evaluated rebalance. The first call on a new meme is always allowed.
2. **Price.** The meme reads its stock's price from the oracle with `get_price_no_older_than` (15 minutes). With no fresh price the outcome is `stale_price` and the cooldown is not used.
3. **TWAP.** mNAV uses the pool's 30-minute average price. A pool younger than that window gives `no_market`, and the cooldown is not used.
4. **Evaluate.** The contract records NAV and mNAV, starts the cooldown, sets the redemption window (open below 0.9×), and takes at most one action:

| mNAV | Outcome | Limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| above 2.0× | `issued`: sells just enough new tokens into the pool to bring the price toward 2.0× NAV. The USDC goes to treasury cash and later buys stock | At most 1% of circulating supply per 24 hours. Only if every new token sells at or above NAV (accretive) |
| 1.0× to 2.0× | `no_action` | |
| below 1.0× | `bought_back`: buys tokens from the pool with cash above the 20% buffer and burns them | Never sells stock |

Every evaluated call logs a `rebalance` event with the caller, `mnav_bps`, NAV, TWAP, `redemption_open`, the outcome and the tip.

## The tip

The caller earns **0.05%** of the USDC value an issuance or buyback moved, capped by treasury cash. The tip is credited as claimable USDC on the meme, not sent. Claim it with **Claim … USDC** in the trade panel (`claim_usdc`).

A call that ends in `no_action` moves nothing and pays no tip.

## Running it from the app

On a meme page, under the mNAV gauge, one line reads: "Anyone can run the rules once an hour, for a 0.05% tip on what they move." On the right it shows one of:

| Shown | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Run rebalance** | The cooldown is over and the oracle price is fresh. Click to call `rebalance()` with 100 TGas |
| Next in … | The cooldown is still running |
| Waits for a fresh … price | The oracle price is older than 15 minutes |
| **Connect to run** | No wallet connected |

After the call, the toast reads "Running the rules", then one of:

| Toast title | Outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Issued new tokens | "Sold … new SYMBOL into the pool for $…, now treasury cash for more TICKER." plus your tip |
| Bought back tokens | "Bought and burned … SYMBOL for $…." plus your tip |
| Rules ran: nothing to do | mNAV was inside the hold zone, or nothing was allowed within the caps and the buffer. "No tip without a move." |
| Nothing ran: stale price | No price within the allowed age. The cooldown is not used |
| Nothing ran: no average price yet | The pool is younger than its average-price window. The cooldown is not used |

When a tip was paid, the toast adds "Your tip, $…, is claimable in the trade panel."

## Running it from the CLI

```bash
near contract call-function as-transaction honey.solidon.testnet rebalance json-args '{}' \
  prepaid-gas '100.0 Tgas' attached-deposit '0 NEAR' sign-as <you>.testnet network-config testnet sign-with-keychain send
```

Read the state first with `get_treasury`: `last_rebalance`, `next_rebalance` (Unix seconds), `last_mnav_bps`, `redemption_open`, `epoch_cap` and `epoch_issued`.

## The keeper

The Solidon keeper (`backend/keeper`) runs on the hosted testnet deployment. For every meme the factory lists, it calls `rebalance()` with 100 TGas whenever the cooldown allows and the oracle price is fresh. It also pushes Chainlink prices to the testnet oracle and runs the treasury steps (`init_treasury`, `approve_router`, `fund_chain`, `buy_stock` and the Solana equivalents).

The keeper holds no special power over the rules. If it stops, anyone can call `rebalance()` and collect the tips. Memes listed in `KEEPER_SKIP_MEMES` are left alone: on the hosted deployment that is `floor.solidon.testnet`, so its rules can be run by hand from the app.
