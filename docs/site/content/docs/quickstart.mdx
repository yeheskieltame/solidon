---
title: "Try it in five minutes"
description: "Open the app, get a funded testnet wallet, buy HONEY, and check its treasury on three chains."
---

This walkthrough uses the hosted app at [app.solidon.xyz](https://app.solidon.xyz). Everything is on NEAR testnet. You need no extension and no real money.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the app

Go to [app.solidon.xyz](https://app.solidon.xyz). The header has three tabs: **Markets**, **Launch** and **Dashboard**. Markets lists every meme the factory `solidon.testnet` launched.

</Step>
<Step>

### Get a funded wallet

<Tabs items={["Demo wallet", "Your own wallet"]}>
<Tab value="Demo wallet">

Click **Try a demo wallet** in the header (or **Connect wallet**, then **Start with a demo wallet**).

- The app generates an ed25519 key in your browser and uses its implicit NEAR testnet account.
- The Solidon faucet creates the account and sends 0.5 NEAR for gas plus 1,000 test USDC. You sign nothing and pay no gas.
- A toast shows "Creating and funding your demo wallet", then "Test NEAR and 1,000 USDC sent", with links to each transaction on NearBlocks.
- The header shows a **Demo** chip with your account. Its menu has **View on NearBlocks**, **Export key**, **Disconnect (keep the key)** and **Forget demo wallet**.

The key lives only in this browser. Transactions sign without popups.

</Tab>
<Tab value="Your own wallet">

Click **Connect wallet** and pick a NEAR wallet under "Or use your own wallet". Use a testnet account.

Then click **Get test funds** in the header. The server-side faucet pays, so you need no NEAR first:

- 0.5 NEAR for gas, only if your account holds less than 0.3 NEAR.
- 1,000 test USDC (`usdc.solidon.testnet`), with storage registration.

The toast reads "Test NEAR and 1,000 USDC sent" or "1,000 test USDC minted". One request per account every 10 minutes.

</Tab>
</Tabs>

You see your USDC balance in the header.

</Step>
<Step>

### Open HONEY

On **Markets**, open **HONEY** (Honeypot, `honey.solidon.testnet`). Its treasury holds NVDA.

You see the price, **mNAV**, **Treasury value**, an mNAV gauge, the trade panel on the right, and "Where the treasury is": one row per chain plus "Cash on NEAR".

</Step>
<Step>

### Buy 10 USDC of HONEY

In the trade panel, stay on **Buy**. "You pay" is prefilled with 10 USDC. The line under it says "You get ≈ … HONEY · fee 1%".

Click **Buy ~… HONEY for 10 USDC**.

- Demo wallet: it signs at once ("Signing").
- Your own wallet: approve in the wallet. The first buy also registers your account on HONEY (0.00125 NEAR storage), so the wallet asks twice.

The toast goes from "Buying HONEY" to "Bought HONEY". Your HONEY balance appears in the panel, and the treasury value rises by the 0.8% treasury fee.

</Step>
<Step>

### See the transaction on the explorer

Click **The transaction on NearBlocks** in the success toast. You see a `ft_transfer_call` on `usdc.solidon.testnet` with receiver `honey.solidon.testnet` and `msg` `{"action":"buy","min_out":"…"}`, then the meme's `buy` event. See [Trade](/docs/guides/trade) for the details.

</Step>
<Step>

### Details: Treasury per chain

Scroll to **Details** and open **Treasury per chain**. For Base Sepolia, Robinhood Chain testnet and Solana devnet you see:

- The treasury address (only this contract can sign for it) and whether the router is approved.
- A proof label: **MPC-proven** (Base) or **Relayer-reported** (Robinhood Chain, Solana).
- **Ledger** (what the contract booked) against **On-chain** (read live by your browser), with a verdict: "Matches", "Covered, +…" or "Short by …".

A chain whose on-chain balance is below the ledger shows "Less on-chain" in "Where the treasury is". HONEY shows it on Base and Robinhood Chain today, from two swaps that reverted. See the [FAQ](/docs/faq).

</Step>
<Step>

### Details: Holders

Open **Holders**. Every holder is read from `ft_balance_of`. Your account is tagged **You**. "Pool reserve" is the tokens held by the contract's own pool.

</Step>
<Step>

### Dashboard

Open **Dashboard**. You see **Portfolio value** (your memes at pool price), **At NAV** (the treasury assets behind your tokens), **USDC**, and **Claimable** (creator fees, caller tips and failed payouts). Each position lists Balance, At pool price, At NAV and Share of treasury.

</Step>
<Step>

### Launch your own meme

Open **Launch**. Name it, pick a ticker and a stock, seed the pool, open the treasury. Three transactions. See [Launch a meme](/docs/guides/launch).

</Step>
</Steps>

## Read the live state without the app

| Do | See |
| --- | --- |
| Open [`solidon.testnet` on NearBlocks](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/address/solidon.testnet) | The factory and the memes it launched |
| `near contract call-function as-read-only honey.solidon.testnet get_treasury_chains json-args '{}' network-config testnet now` | HONEY's treasury per chain: address, nonce, USDC, NVDA booked, cash in transit |
| Open [api.solidon.xyz/health](https://api.solidon.xyz/health) | The read API is up |
